Supplement · field map

The six-role landscape template is common.
Citation graphs are not.

Ding 2019 × Fard 2017 is the adjudicated specimen, not the population. Four substrate clusters fill the same slots; the missing transfers are the paper’s opportunity set.
Read this figure as an interface, not an identity claim. Clusters share a role template (state, representation, scalar field, transition, trajectory rule, feedback). They almost never share a mathematical object. Solid arrows = routine within-cluster flow. Dashed crimson arrows = transfers that topic-search and citation tools miss.

Missing transfers (what to productize)

1 → 2. Learned nonparametric J (GP / VAE likelihood / EVE index) grafted onto explicit T from RNA velocity, Hopfield update, or geometric landscape ODEs. Test: do inferred minima of J coincide with fate attractors on a system that has both DMS-like labels and trajectories.
1 ↔ 2 via u. Drug dose and morphogen concentration are the same slot. Resistance seascapes and fate landscapes should share the time-dependent potential, not just the word “landscape.”
1 ↔ 3. Sequence rewires the folding funnel; measured fitness is often a functional of that energy surface. Variant-effect models and frustration maps belong in one object.
2 ↔ 4. Fard’s quadratic Lyapunov energy is the classical Hopfield object. Modern Hopfield capacity and learned Lyapunov certificates are the upgrade path for cell-fate models.
1 ↔ 4. Adaptive walks on empirical fitness surfaces and SGD on loss surfaces are the same local-search-plus-noise template (epistasis ≈ nonconvexity).
3 ↔ 4. Funnel geometry vs loss-basin geometry. Visualization and Hessian tools from deep learning are underused on folding and fitness surfaces.
